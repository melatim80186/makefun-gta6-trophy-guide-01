Roadmap to the Platinum

Work the stages in order. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops the moment the last trophy is earned. Estimated total: around 200 hours.

  1. 1

    Story first — Chapters 1–4 & finale

    Play the story straight through. Chapter trophies (Paradise Lost through Top Of The World, Ma), the mission trophies (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) and the finale all pop as you go. Do the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends — it may not come back.

  2. 2

    Stranger strands

    Finish 8 stranger mission strands (Strangers & Freaks) — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret all have finales. The strands stay open after the story, so there is no rush, but they unlock some of the best side content.

  3. 3

    Activities & combat

    Stunts (250), Shooting Range golds (42), races (20 different courses plus the Top Fun drone races), fishing against Cal, the arcade, bowling, challenges (50), Combat Focus, robberies (shops, vaults, armored trucks, jewelry stores, gang operations) and the money goals ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent).

  4. 4

    Collectibles & exploration

    Card sets (3), WAiNK spots (100), vehicles (100), meditation spots, all garages, and exploring every corner of Leonida — Vice City, Vice Beach, Port Gellhorn, Mount Kalaga and the Leonida Keys.

  5. 5

    Cleanup → 100% → Platinum

    Check the in-game stats for whatever is left, finish it for A Long Road (100% completion), and Vice Got a Price! is yours.

⚠ Missable trophies

Only one trophy has evidence of being missable: Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (the morning after “Down to Earth”) may disappear after Chapter 1, and there is no mission replay. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save. Every other trophy can be earned after the story. The 100% checklist needs only 15 of 18 meditations, so whether all 18 are required here is still unconfirmed at launch — sources: GTABase, OutputLag.
